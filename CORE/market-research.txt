One thing you need to know
The brief had the wrong statute. RCW 59.18.020 is not the security deposit law. It is "Rights and remedies — Obligation of good faith imposed." I verified this against the statute text before writing the page.

The correct sections are RCW 59.18.280 (statement and refund, the ordinary-wear bar, the penalties) and RCW 59.18.260 (deposit conditions, move-in checklist). The page cites 59.18.280. Citing 59.18.020 as deposit law on a page whose whole value is legal credibility would have been the kind of error a competitor screenshots.
