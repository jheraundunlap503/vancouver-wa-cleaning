Our Story

Rooted in the Northwest Freshness

GreenCleaning Vancouver began with a simple belief: our homes should feel as crisp and restorative as the Pacific Northwest air. As a family-owned business, we started by serving our neighbors in Kitsilano, bringing a personal touch and eco-conscious methods to every home we entered.

Today, we’ve grown to serve the broader Vancouver community, but our core remains unchanged. We don't just clean spaces; we restore tranquility. We rely on rigorous standards, sustainable practices, and a team that genuinely cares about the wellbeing of your environment.

Sarah Jenkins

Founder, GreenCleaning
