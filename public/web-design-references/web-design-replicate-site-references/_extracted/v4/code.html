Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries about our premium services.

eco Are your cleaning products safe for pets?

Yes, we prioritize the "Northwest Freshness" standard. All our standard products are eco-friendly, non-toxic, and perfectly safe for households with pets and small children.

event_available Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Not at all. Many of our clients provide access via lockbox or keypad. Our professional teams are fully vetted, insured, and handle your property with the utmost respect.

verified What is included in a standard clean?

A standard clean covers dusting all accessible surfaces, wiping down kitchen and bathroom counters, sweeping/mopping hard floors, and vacuuming carpets. Detailed checklists are available upon request.

schedule How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least one week in advance to secure your preferred date and time, especially for deep cleans or move-in/out services.
