## Demographics
- Median household income: $141,126
- Average home value: $711,000 to $789,000
- Owner occupied: 78.3 percent
- Local cleaning labor rate: $31 per hour

## Housing Stock
Predominantly newer, larger single family construction. High share of custom and
semi custom built homes relative to the rest of Clark County. The owner occupied
rate, well above the county average, supports a recurring service model over
one time or move out cleaning as the lead offer.

## Angle
Recurring maid service, premium positioning. Lead with the custom finishes
(quartz, natural stone, hardwood) and the care they require, not with price.
Pricing runs above the Vancouver average and should be framed as a function of
home size and finish quality, not padded margin.
