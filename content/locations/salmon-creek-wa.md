## Why Salmon Creek Is a Recurring Service Market

Salmon Creek is unincorporated Clark County, not a City of Vancouver neighborhood, even though most of it carries a Vancouver mailing address. That distinction does not change how a visit is scheduled, but it is worth knowing because the area runs on its own identity, built around Klineline Pond, the Legacy Salmon Creek medical district, and Washington State University Vancouver rather than downtown Vancouver.

What does change how a visit is scheduled is how long people actually stay. [86.8 percent of Salmon Creek residents were living in the same house a year earlier](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/salmoncreekcdpwashington), and 78.7 percent of housing units are owner occupied, both well above what a high-turnover rental market would show. Median household income runs well above the Washington state average on the same page. Put together, that is a profile of people who are not shopping for a one time rescue clean, they are looking for a crew to maintain a house they already know well and plan to keep living in.

## What a Recurring Visit Covers in a Salmon Creek Home

Every visit is the same list, which is what makes it possible to learn a house and get faster at it rather than starting over each time.

### Kitchen

Counters and backsplash, sink and faucet, exterior of every appliance, stovetop and control panel, microwave interior, cabinet fronts at hand height, table, and floor.

### Bathrooms

Toilet inside and out including the base, shower and tub surround, glass, sink and counter, mirror, fixtures, and floor. Towels straightened, trash out.

### Living areas and bedrooms

Dusting of all reachable surfaces and sills, mirrors and glass, vacuum on carpet, sweep and mop on hard floors, and the particular spots that wear faster in a house that has been lived in for years rather than months, the hallway runner, the spot by the back door, the stair treads.

### Every visit, not just some

Trash and recycling out, high touch surfaces disinfected in the kitchen and bathrooms, and a walk through at the end.

## Serving Long-Time Owners and the WSU Vancouver Community

Most of Salmon Creek is settled, owner occupied, and built around daily life rather than new construction, which is a different crowd than a subdivision still closing new homes every month. A consistent day and a consistent crew tend to matter more here than anywhere else in Clark County, since the client relationship is often measured in years rather than a single job.

Salmon Creek is also home to [Washington State University Vancouver](https://events.columbian.com/event/wsu-vancouver-preview-day/), whose campus sits on NE Salmon Creek Ave, and the off campus rental housing that comes with any university. That means two different jobs inside the same service area: a recurring schedule for a family that has owned the house for a decade, and a single visit or move out clean for a rental near campus. Both get handled by the same crews, just scoped differently.

[Klineline Pond at Salmon Creek Regional Park](https://clark.wa.gov/public-works/salmon-creek-regional-park/klineline-pond), with its swim beach and the start of the Salmon Creek Greenway Trail, is the area's best known landmark and a reasonable way to describe where a job is relative to the rest of Clark County.

Recurring visits in Salmon Creek run the same $150 to $260 range as the rest of the Vancouver area, priced by square footage, bathroom count, and how long it has been since the house was properly cleaned, not by the neighborhood.
