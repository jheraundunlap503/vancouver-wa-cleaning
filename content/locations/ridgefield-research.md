## Growth
- 6.81 percent annual growth, fastest growing city in Washington State
- Growth driven by master-planned subdivisions under continuous construction
- Constant pipeline of brand new, never-occupied homes closing every month

## Housing Stock
New construction dominates. Builder-grade finish work (drywall dust, paint
overspray, protective film on floors, windows, and appliances, caulk haze
on glass) is the default condition of a home at handoff, not an exception.

## Demand Signal
Move-in cleaning and post construction cleanup, not recurring maintenance,
are the first thing most Ridgefield homeowners need. Recurring service
becomes relevant only after the household has settled in.

## Angle
Move-in cleaning, post construction cleanup, new homeowner market. Lead
with what builders leave behind and why a standard clean does not remove
it, not with price.
