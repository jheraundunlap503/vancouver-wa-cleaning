# Salmon Creek, WA — Location Research

Research artifact. Not published. Verify every UNCERTAIN item before it reaches a page.

Jurisdiction note: Salmon Creek is an unincorporated Clark County census
designated place (CDP), not a City of Vancouver neighborhood, despite
carrying Vancouver mailing addresses. cityofvancouver.us has no
jurisdictional data for it. Use clark.wa.gov, census.gov, and columbian.com.

---

## Median Household Income

$111,631 (2020-2024 American Community Survey 5 year estimate, 2024
dollars). Source: [U.S. Census Bureau QuickFacts, Salmon Creek CDP, Washington](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/salmoncreekcdpwashington).

## Home Value

Median value of owner-occupied housing units is $530,100. The brief asked
for "average home value," but the Census publishes a median, not an
average. Source: same QuickFacts page as above.

## Housing Stock Type

UNCERTAIN. The single-family versus multifamily unit breakdown lives in
ACS Table DP04, which Census QuickFacts does not surface at the CDP level
and which was not retrievable in this pass. Do not state a specific
housing-type split on the page. What is verifiable instead: 86.8 percent
of residents age 1 and older were living in the same house a year earlier
(2020-2024 ACS), and 78.7 percent of units are owner-occupied, both of
which point to settled, long-tenure households rather than describing
whether those households are in single-family homes, townhomes, or
multifamily buildings.

## Owner-Occupied vs. Rental

78.7 percent of housing units are owner-occupied, which puts the renter
share at approximately 21.3 percent (21.3 derived by subtraction, not an
independently published figure). Source: same QuickFacts page as above.

## HOA Density

UNCERTAIN, and likely unanswerable from public data. Clark County
publishes a neighborhood association directory, but the county's own
definition treats neighborhood associations as a civic-participation
structure, not a homeowners association with covenants, dues, or
maintenance authority. There is no county-published count of actual HOAs
for Salmon Creek. Do not report a specific HOA count or density figure.

## Conditions Relevant to Cleaning Demand

The strongest verified signal is demographic, not climatic. Salmon Creek's
population is 22.1 percent age 65 and over, against 86.8 percent of
residents living in the same house as a year earlier, both from the same
[QuickFacts page](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/salmoncreekcdpwashington).
Combined with the 78.7 percent owner-occupied rate and a median household
income well above the Washington state median, that is a profile of
established, higher-income households who have been in their homes for a
while, not a renter-turnover market. That argues for recurring maintenance
cleaning as the primary demand driver, rather than move-out or short term
turnover work.

No verified precipitation, mold, or moss figure specific to Salmon Creek
was retrievable in this pass (the nearest weather station data is for
Vancouver/Pearson Field, not Salmon Creek itself). Do not state a specific
rainfall or climate figure on the page. The general Pacific Northwest damp
season point already used on other pages (see deep-cleaning-vancouver-wa.md,
citing epa.gov on moisture control) still applies qualitatively and can be
reused without inventing a Salmon Creek specific number.

Explicit prohibition: Clark County's Public Health program lists Clark
County as an EPA Zone 1 (highest) radon potential area. This is real and
verifiable, but it has no connection to house cleaning. Radon mitigation is
a distinct service performed by licensed contractors, not something a
cleaning visit addresses. Do not mention radon on the page. Including it
would imply a false health claim about what cleaning service accomplishes.

## Landmarks (3, Google Maps Recognized)

1. [Klineline Pond / Salmon Creek Regional Park](https://clark.wa.gov/public-works/salmon-creek-regional-park/klineline-pond), a Clark County park with a swim beach, splash pad, and fishing platforms, and the starting point of the Salmon Creek Greenway Trail.
2. The Legacy Salmon Creek medical district, described in Clark County's own [Highway 99 Sub-Area Plan](https://clark.wa.gov/sites/default/files/dept/files/community-planning/projects/Highway%2099/FINAL_SUB-AREA_PLAN%20WITH%20COVER.pdf) as a retail and office hub built around the hospital. Confirm the hospital's current operating name directly before publishing, since ownership and naming of hospitals changes.
3. [Washington State University Vancouver](https://events.columbian.com/event/wsu-vancouver-preview-day/), with its campus address at 14204 NE Salmon Creek Ave, Vancouver, WA 98686, per Columbian event listings.

Maps-recognized status itself is not independently verifiable from these
sources. All three are real, physically located in Salmon Creek, and
commonly used as the area's identifying landmarks.
