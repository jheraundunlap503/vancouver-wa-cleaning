## House Cleaning Services in Vancouver, WA

Three jobs make up most of the calls that come in from Vancouver and the rest of Clark County. They are different work at different prices, and picking the wrong one is why people end up disappointed.

### Move Out Cleaning Vancouver WA

Your lease ends, or you are closing on a sale, and somebody is about to walk through the place with a checklist and a strong opinion about your deposit. Empty rooms hide nothing. This is the most detailed clean there is, and it is the one with money attached to the outcome. See what a [move out clean covers and what Washington law says about deposits](/services/move-out-cleaning-vancouver-wa/).

### Deep Cleaning Vancouver WA

The house is not filthy, it has just gotten away from you. Grout has gone gray, the shower door has a film on it, and there is a layer on top of the fridge you have been ignoring since spring. A [deep clean resets the house](/services/deep-cleaning-vancouver-wa/) so that normal cleaning works again.

### Recurring Maid Service Vancouver WA

You do not want to think about this again. Weekly, every other week, or monthly, same crew, same day, house handled. [Recurring maid service](/services/recurring-maid-service-vancouver-wa/) is the cheapest per visit because the house never gets far enough gone to need rescuing.

We also handle Airbnb and short term rental turnovers and post construction cleanup after remodels and new builds.

## What House Cleaning Costs in Vancouver, WA

Nobody can quote a house sight unseen and mean it. What follows is the range most Vancouver homes land in, and the questions that move a price inside that range.

| Type of clean | Typical range | What drives the price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recurring, every other week | 150 to 260 per visit | Square footage, bathrooms, pets |
| Deep clean | 300 to 600 | Time since last deep clean, grout and glass condition |
| Move out clean | 350 to 650 | Empty or furnished, appliance interiors, carpet condition |
| Airbnb turnover | 95 to 185 per turn | Bed count, linens, restocking |
| Post construction | 350 to 1,200 | Square footage, drywall dust, remodel or full build |

Three things move a quote more than anything else. Bathrooms, because they take the longest per square foot. Pets, because hair gets everywhere and doubles vacuum time. And how long it has been, because a house that has never been deep cleaned needs one before recurring service means anything.

### Why the first visit costs more

On recurring plans the first visit is priced as a deep clean, then the rate drops. That is not an upsell. A crew cannot maintain a house in two hours if there are two years of buildup on the baseboards, so the first visit does that work once and every visit after it is maintenance.

## How Booking a House Cleaner in Vancouver Works

### Walkthrough and quote

You call, describe the house, and get a price. Square footage, bathrooms, pets, and last cleaning date are enough to quote accurately for most homes. Nothing gets scheduled before you know the number.

### The clean

The crew arrives in the agreed window with supplies and equipment. You do not need to be there. Access happens by key, lockbox, or garage code, arranged in advance.

### Final inspection

You look at the work. If something got missed, it gets fixed. On move out cleans this matters most, because the next person through the door is a landlord with a checklist.

## Cleaning Problems Specific to the Pacific Northwest

Cleaning a house here is not the same job as cleaning one in Phoenix. Three things make it different.

### Winter rain, mud, and entryway tracking

From October to May, everything that walks in the door is wet. Entryways, hallway runners, and the first six feet of every floor take the damage, and they need attention on a schedule that has nothing to do with the rest of the house.

### Damp season mildew in bathrooms and window frames

This is the one people underestimate. Mold is a moisture problem before it is a cleaning problem, and the EPA is direct about it: the way to control indoor mold growth is to [control moisture rather than try to eliminate spores](https://www.epa.gov/mold/what-are-main-ways-control-moisture-your-home), keeping indoor relative humidity under 60 percent and ideally between 30 and 50. In a Vancouver winter that means running the bathroom fan longer than feels necessary, venting the dryer outside, and staying ahead of the film that builds on shower glass, grout, and the bottom of window frames. Cleaning treats the symptom. Ventilation is what actually fixes it.

### Spring pollen and indoor air

Late spring loads every horizontal surface in the house with a fine yellow layer, and it comes back within days of being wiped. Sills, blinds, ceiling fan blades, and vent covers are where it collects and where it gets missed.

### How often a house here actually needs cleaning

The American Cleaning Institute's baseline is that living space floors get [vacuumed or swept and mopped weekly](https://www.cleaninginstitute.org/cleaning-tips/cleaning-schedule), with deep cleaning handled a few times a year rather than weekly. Their point about frequency is the useful part: the more consistently a house gets cleaned, the less deep cleaning it needs to catch up. Houses on every other week schedules almost never need another full deep clean after the first one.
