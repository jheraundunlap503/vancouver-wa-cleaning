## What Move Out Cleaning Covers in Vancouver, WA

An empty unit is a different job than an occupied one. There is no furniture to clean around and nothing hiding the parts of the room that have not seen daylight in three years. That is the whole point, and it is also why the scope is longer than any other clean.

### Kitchen

Inside the oven, inside the refrigerator, inside and on top of every cabinet, the range hood and filter, behind and underneath the range and refrigerator where they roll out, drawer tracks, and the seals around the dishwasher door. Appliance interiors are where inspections start.

### Bathrooms

Grout, shower glass and its track, under the toilet base and behind it, inside the vanity, the exhaust fan cover, and the mineral film on fixtures that most people never notice until it is pointed out on a walkthrough.

### Living areas and bedrooms

Baseboards, door frames, light switch plates, closet shelving and rods, window interiors and sills, window tracks, blinds, ceiling fans, and vent covers. Walls get spot cleaned where scuffs come off without taking paint with them.

### Everything the furniture was covering

Floor corners, the perimeter of every room, behind doors, inside the linen closet, and the top edge of every door. None of this matters in an occupied home and all of it is visible in an empty one.

## What Washington Law Says About Your Deposit

This is the part worth understanding before you negotiate with anyone, because most deposit disputes are lost by tenants who did not know the rules rather than by tenants who left a dirty apartment.

### Ordinary use is not chargeable

The controlling statute is RCW 59.18.280, and it says plainly that a landlord may not charge a tenant for [wear resulting from ordinary use of the premises](https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=59.18.280), or for damages that are not substantiated by documentation. Traffic patterns in carpet, faded paint, and the ordinary softening of a unit over a tenancy are not deductible. Grease baked onto an oven floor is a different category, and that is the category a move out clean removes.

There is a second half to that provision worth knowing. Unsubstantiated charges also may not be reported to a consumer reporting agency, a tenant screening service, or a prospective landlord, or sent to collections.

### The statement and the deadline

Within 30 days of the tenancy ending and the unit being vacated, the landlord has to give you a full and specific written statement of the basis for keeping any part of the deposit, along with copies of the estimates or paid invoices that substantiate the charges, and any refund owed. Some older tenant guides still say 21 days. The statute text says 30, so work from the statute.

If the landlord does not comply, the statute makes them liable for the full deposit, and a court may award up to two times the deposit where the refusal to provide the statement or refund was intentional. The prevailing party can also recover costs and reasonable attorney fees.

### Carpet cleaning is not automatic

A landlord cannot deduct for carpet cleaning as a matter of routine. It has to be documented wear beyond ordinary use, like any other charge. A blanket carpet cleaning fee taken out of every departing tenant's deposit is exactly the kind of charge the documentation requirement exists to stop.

### What "professionally cleaned" means, and what it does not

Washington does not define a professional cleaning standard that a landlord can hold you to, and no statute requires you to hire a company or produce a receipt. What the law actually cares about is condition and documentation. A lease clause demanding professional carpet cleaning does not override the statute's ordinary use protection.

The practical defense is a record. The Attorney General's guidance on [deposits and move in condition checklists](https://www.atg.wa.gov/deposits-and-fees) makes the point that the checklist signed by both parties at move in is what a condition claim gets measured against later. Photograph the unit after the clean, date stamped, every room.

Three things move a quote inside that range. Whether the unit is already empty, since cleaning around a stack of boxes costs time. The condition of the oven, which is the single largest variable in the kitchen. And pets, because hair in carpet edges and baseboard corners is slow work.

## When to Book

Leases in Clark County overwhelmingly end on the last day of the month, which means the last four or five days of every month are the busiest of the cycle and they book out first. The end of June is worse than any other week of the year.

Two weeks of notice is comfortable. Three days before a month end handover is not, and that is the call that comes in most often.

Book the clean for after the movers have finished and before the walkthrough, with at least a day of margin. If something needs a second pass, that day is what makes it possible.

## What Clark County Landlords Actually Check

Property managers running multiple units work from a standard checklist, and the order rarely changes. Oven interior. Refrigerator interior, including the seals and the drawers. Inside every cabinet and drawer. Bathroom grout and the toilet base. Window tracks. Baseboards. Closet shelving. Blinds.

That list is the reason move out cleaning is priced differently than a deep clean. It is not a better clean, it is a clean written against somebody else's document.
