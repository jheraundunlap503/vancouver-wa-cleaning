## What a Turnover Covers

A turnover is not a smaller house clean. It is a reset against a checklist, run under a clock, and finished with a photo set.

### Every turn

Beds stripped and remade to a presentation standard, bathrooms reset including a fresh towel set, kitchen returned to empty and wiped down including inside the microwave and the coffee maker, floors vacuumed and mopped, trash and recycling out, high touch surfaces disinfected, consumables restocked, and everything put back where the listing photos show it.

### The part guests notice

Hair. It is the single most common complaint in short term rental reviews and it collects in exactly four places: the shower drain surround, the bathroom floor edge, the bed frame rails, and the couch seams. Those four get checked every single turn regardless of what else is on the list.

### The photo set

Photographs at the end of every turnover, timestamped. This is how you prove condition at handover if a guest claims pre existing damage, and it is how you see the unit without driving to it.

## Cleaning Between Guests Is Not the Same as Disinfecting

Worth being precise about, because it is easy to over promise in a listing description and get held to it.

Cleaning [removes soil and grease from a surface](https://www.cleaninginstitute.org/cleaning-levels). Sanitizing reduces germ counts to a level public health codes treat as safe. Disinfecting inactivates pathogens. They are three different things, and a surface has to be cleaned before a disinfectant can do anything useful, because dirt shields whatever is underneath it.

On a turnover, everything gets cleaned and the high touch inventory gets disinfected after that: door handles, light switches, remotes, faucet and toilet handles, the fridge door, the kettle, the coffee maker. Writing "fully sanitized" in a listing when what happened was a normal clean is a claim a guest can call you on.

## The Permit Situation in Vancouver

This trips up new hosts, especially the ones who bought in Vancouver expecting Clark County rules.

Inside Vancouver city limits, a [short term rental permit is required](https://www.cityofvancouver.us/government/community-development/short-term-rentals-str/), alongside a Washington State business license carrying your UBI and a city endorsement. The city defines a short term rental as a unit rented for fewer than 30 consecutive nights, sets a one time permit fee, requires liability coverage of at least one million dollars, and requires you to give written courtesy notice to abutting and adjacent property owners before you apply.

Unincorporated Clark County, Camas, Washougal, Battle Ground, and Ridgefield each set their own rules. A property two blocks outside the city line is under a different regime than one two blocks inside it. Check the jurisdiction the parcel actually sits in rather than the mailing address, which is not always the same thing.

## Scheduling Around Your Calendar

Turnovers are booked off your calendar rather than off individual phone calls. Share a calendar link and turns get slotted as bookings land, which is what stops the 9pm realization that tomorrow is a same day turn.

Two practical notes about this market. Summer weekends book out first, particularly anything on the waterfront or within reach of the Gorge. And the tightest windows of the year are the Friday and Sunday turns in July and August, which need to be locked in weeks ahead rather than days.
