## Why Construction Dust Is a Different Job

A house full of drywall dust does not need a bigger version of a normal clean. It needs a different method, and the reason is particle size.

Drywall dust is gypsum ground to a powder far finer than household dust. It stays suspended in the air for hours after the work stops, then settles onto every surface including the ones nobody thinks about, like the top edge of a door, the inside of a light fixture, and the return air vent. Wipe it with a dry cloth and you put most of it back into the air, where it lands again somewhere else. That is the loop people get stuck in.

The method that works is vacuuming with real filtration before anything gets wiped, top to bottom so gravity works in your favor, then repeat passes on subsequent days as the remaining airborne load settles. A single visit on the day the contractor leaves will look finished that afternoon and disappointing the next morning. Anyone promising a one pass result on a full remodel has not done many of them.

## Rough Clean and Final Clean

| | Rough clean | Final clean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| When | Mid project, debris out, before finish work | After the punch list is complete |
| Purpose | Let trades work in a clean space | Make the space livable |
| Includes | Bulk debris, gross dust, floor sweep | Full detail, fixtures, glass, interiors |
| Usually booked by | General contractor | Homeowner |

Most homeowners doing a kitchen or bathroom remodel only need the final. On a full renovation or a new build, a rough clean between phases saves money on the final one.

## What a Final Clean Covers

Ceilings and light fixtures, walls wiped where finish allows, every horizontal surface top to bottom, inside all cabinets and drawers, appliance interiors and exteriors, window interiors including tracks and sills, door tops and frames, baseboards and trim, switch plates and outlet covers, vent and return covers, and floors given the pass their finish requires. New tile grout gets its haze removed. Adhesive residue, stickers, and paint flecks come off glass and fixtures.

## If the House Was Built Before 1978

This is the part worth reading properly rather than skimming, and it applies to a lot of housing stock in central Vancouver.

Any renovation, repair, or painting that disturbs painted surfaces in a pre 1978 home can create lead dust. The EPA's Renovation, Repair and Painting Rule requires that anyone paid to perform such work in a pre 1978 home [be certified and trained in lead safe work practices](https://www.epa.gov/lead/lead-renovation-repair-and-painting-program), and the rule's requirements include specific practices for containing dust and for cleaning the work area afterward. The purpose of the rule is [minimizing exposure to lead based paint dust](https://www.epa.gov/lead/what-purpose-renovation-repair-and-painting-rrp-rule) during and after the work. Homeowners doing the work themselves in their own home are outside the rule, but anyone paid to do it is not.

What that means practically. Ask your contractor for their certification before the work starts, not after. If the work is already done and you do not know whether the crew followed lead safe practices, say so when you book the cleaning, because it changes the method and it is not a detail to discover halfway through.

You can check a contractor's registration and workers compensation standing in Washington through the [Labor and Industries verification tool](https://lni.wa.gov/licensing-permits/contractors/hiring-a-contractor/verify-contractor-tradesperson-business), which is worth doing before the remodel rather than after it.

## Timing Around Your Punch List

Book the clean after the punch list is done. Every time somebody schedules it earlier to save a week, the painter comes back for one touch up and the dust returns.

New construction in Ridgefield, Battle Ground, and the north county subdivisions runs on a predictable cycle, and the building season here peaks from late spring through September while the weather holds. That is also when cleaning crews are hardest to book at short notice. If your completion date is in that window, get on a schedule early and move it later if the build slips, which builds do.
